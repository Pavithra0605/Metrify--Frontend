About METRIFY

Smart Digital Verification Platform for Measuring Instruments.

Project Vision & Overview

METRIFY is developed as a college engineering project to modernize metrology compliance and measuring instrument tracking for industries, calibration laboratories, and regulatory bodies. By transitioning from paper certificates to secure digital records with QR traceability, METRIFY ensures precision, accountability, and real-time audit readiness.

Core Modules & Features

  • Instrument Registry: Centralized database tracking serial numbers, categories, owners, and calibration cycles.
  • Digital Verification: Instant ID lookup and QR-ready verification ensuring validity against calibration standards.
  • Inspection Management: End-to-end audit logging, inspector sign-offs, and automated status updates.
  • Role-Based Dashboard: Real-time statistics on active, verified, pending, and expired instruments with due-date alert monitoring.
